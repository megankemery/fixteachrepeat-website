The Outfit Lineup

10 easy looks to steal this fall

The best part is that these are built from the same kinds of staple pieces, so you can mix them with things you probably already own.

1

Cozy Sweater + Wide Leg Jeans

A dusty rose sweater with relaxed light-wash jeans is an easy everyday combination that still looks intentional. Add simple sneakers and a structured tote.

Cozy • Casual • Everyday
2

Long Cardigan + Leggings

Black leggings and a simple white top get instantly more polished with a long cardigan. White sneakers keep the whole thing comfortable.

Errands • Travel • Busy Days
3

Casual Henley + Ripped Jeans

A soft henley with relaxed denim is one of those outfits that works without much thought. Add a tote and neutral shoes and call it done.

Weekend • Coffee Run • Casual
4

Plaid Shacket + Leggings

An oversized plaid layer makes basic black leggings feel much more like an outfit. Keep the underneath simple and let the shacket do the work.

Layered • Relaxed • Fall
5

Pullover + Black Flare Leggings

A quarter-zip or pullover with black flare leggings hits that sweet spot between sporty and put together. Add a crossbody for hands-free real life.

Sporty • Comfortable • Easy
6

Striped Sweater + Wide Leg Pants

A striped knit with soft wide-leg pants feels comfortable enough for home but polished enough to wear out. Neutral sneakers keep it casual.

Comfy • Cute • Relaxed
7

Olive Cardigan + Straight Leg Jeans

Olive is an easy fall neutral. Layer a longer cardigan over a basic top and straight-leg jeans for a simple outfit that still has some color.

Casual • Layered • Neutral
8

Quarter Zip + Leggings

A dusty rose quarter-zip, black leggings, sneakers, and a crossbody make an easy outfit for days when comfort is non-negotiable.

On-the-Go • Sporty • Comfortable
9

Camel Cardigan + Wide Leg Pants

A cream basic top, camel cardigan, and chocolate wide-leg pants give you a richer fall palette without making the outfit complicated.

Work • Polished • Fall Neutral
10

Ribbed Sweater + Relaxed Jeans

A textured sweater with easy denim is the kind of outfit you can repeat all season. Swap shoes, jewelry, or the bag to change the feel.

Everyday • Repeatable • Easy
