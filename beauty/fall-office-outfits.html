The Workweek Lineup

10 office looks that still feel like you

These outfits are built around repeatable basics: good trousers, comfortable knits, simple layers, and pieces that can actually survive a full workday.

1

Forest Green Sweater + Black Trousers

A deep green sweater with black wide-leg trousers is simple, polished, and easy to repeat. Add a belt and structured tote for an office-ready finish.

Classic • Easy • Polished
2

Green Blazer + Camel Trousers

A dark blazer instantly dresses up a basic black top. Camel trousers soften the look and make the whole outfit feel more interesting than standard black-on-black.

Meeting • Business Casual • Classic
3

Striped Sweater + Green Trousers

A striped knit keeps tailored trousers from feeling too formal. This is an easy way to bring personality into a work outfit while still looking put together.

Comfortable • Classic • Everyday
4

Button Cardigan + Black Pants

A fitted cardigan works almost like a blouse. Pair it with black trousers and loafers for a look that feels polished without requiring a blazer.

Simple • Office • Comfortable
5

Black Turtleneck + Olive Wide Leg Pants

A black fitted top with olive trousers gives you an easy fall color palette that still works beautifully in a professional setting.

Sleek • Fall • Professional
6

Cream Cardigan + Black Trousers

A soft cream cardigan lightens up black trousers and gives you an easy layer for offices that somehow manage to stay freezing year-round.

Layered • Neutral • Workday
7

Green Blouse + Black Pants

A simple blouse with black trousers is a no-fuss office formula. Add simple jewelry and heels or loafers depending on how dressed-up your workplace is.

Feminine • Polished • Easy
8

Long Cardigan + Straight Jeans

For casual-office days, pair straight-leg denim with a basic top and long cardigan. The longer layer helps the outfit still feel intentional.

Casual Friday • Layered • Relaxed
9

White Button Down + Olive Trousers

A crisp white shirt and olive trousers is one of those combinations that works almost anywhere. Add leopard or neutral shoes for a little personality.

Timeless • Workwear • Polished
10

Ribbed Knit Dress + Boots

A knit midi dress is the one-piece answer to "I don't know what to wear." Add ankle boots, simple jewelry, and a tote.

One-and-Done • Feminine • Fall
